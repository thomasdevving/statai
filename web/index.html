THE CHEF’S METHOD. YOUR OWN HANDS.

A lesson that follows your side of the board.

Reference-led guidance,
one visible step at a time.

  1. 01

    See what the chef means.

    Each lesson pairs short instructions with reference frames and visible success criteria. You follow a specific method, with the next step close at hand.

  2. 02

    Check. Adjust. Keep going.

    Compare your camera view with the current step. Get a pass, a specific correction, or “can’t tell” when the board isn’t clear. The watcher looks for the lesson’s known mistakes between checks.

  3. 03

    Ask about the lesson.

    The voice flow uses the lesson’s instructions and narration as context for spoken questions. Hands-free controls and the glasses experience are being integrated and tested on iOS.

FEEDBACK YOU CAN ACT ON

Echo focuses on what a camera can show: coverage, placement and alignment. For the sandwich lesson, that means spotting filling piled in the middle and guiding it toward the edges.
